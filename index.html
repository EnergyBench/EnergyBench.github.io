Open benchmark · energy time-series forecasting

Benchmarking forecasting models on live energy data

EnergyBench evaluates zero-shot time-series foundation models, task-trained deep networks and statistical baselines on load, solar, wind, electricity-price and fuel-market series from system operators, power exchanges and statistical agencies, under a single protocol and on test data published after the models' training cut-off.

08Models

Models

Foundation models are applied zero-shot, deep models are trained per task, and statistical models are re-estimated at every forecast origin. Selecting a row opens the model's profile.

09Method

Evaluation protocol

MASE

Error of the median forecast, scaled by the in-sample error of repeating the last season (period m), averaged over all scored windows W.

WQL

Pinball loss over the quantiles Q = 0.1, 0.2, …, 0.9, pooled over all windows and normalised by the size of the target. It approximates CRPS.

Relative score

Each task's score is divided by Seasonal Naive's, and the ratios are averaged geometrically over the tasks T of a slice. Below 1 beats the baseline.
